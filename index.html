01 About

I am currently a Ph.D. student (2019–present) at Tsinghua University, advised by Prof. Changshui Zhang.

In 2019, I received my bachelor's degree from the Tsien Excellence in Education Program (Qian class) in the School of Aerospace, Tsinghua University.

My research focuses on algorithmic fairness, robust learning, federated learning and trustworthy AI.
