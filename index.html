About

I received my Ph.D. from the Department of Automation at Tsinghua University in 2024, advised by Prof. Changshui Zhang, where I am now a Research Assistant and Shuimu Scholar. I received my bachelor's degree from the Tsien Excellence in Education Program (Qian class) in the School of Aerospace, Tsinghua University, in 2019. I am also a visiting scholar at the RIKEN Center for Advanced Intelligence Project, working with Prof. Masashi Sugiyama.

My research focuses on algorithmic fairness, robust learning, federated learning, and trustworthy AI. My work aims to build machine learning systems that are fair, robust, and reliable when deployed in real-world, high-stakes applications.

I have published papers at top-tier venues including NeurIPS, ICML, KDD, ICLR, and IEEE TPAMI, and served as a reviewer for major machine learning conferences and journals.

Research

My research interests include:

  • World Models — learning predictive and generative models of the physical world that support perception, reasoning, planning, and simulation for intelligent agents.
  • Embodied Intelligence — developing AI systems that perceive, reason, and act in the physical world, integrating vision, language, and control for embodied agents and robots.
  • Trustworthy LLMs — improving the reliability, fairness, and safety of large language and foundation models, addressing hallucination, alignment, and robust real-world deployment.

Research Lab — Physical RSI

Our mission is to build physical AGI: AI systems that perceive, understand, simulate, and act in the physical world as naturally as they reason in the digital one. Towards this end, we push the frontiers of embodied intelligence through the convergence of world models, robot learning, and foundation-model scaling. Our research agenda centers on three thrusts:

  • 🌀 Research Thrust 1 — World Models: How can a world model faithfully simulate the operation of the entire physical world? World Simulation, Physical Dynamics, Predictive Models, Causal Discovery, Digital Twins, Real2Sim2Real
  • 🤖 Research Thrust 2 — Embodied RSI Systems: How can we efficiently build physical AGI systems that perceive, reason, and act in the real world? Embodied Intelligence, Robot Learning, System Integration, Real-World Deployment
  • 🦾 Research Thrust 3 — Embodied Foundation Models: How can we design model architectures that scale efficiently into physical foundation models? Scalable Architectures, Vision-Language-Action, Data Scaling, Model Scaling, Physical Grounding

💡 We believe the three thrusts are deeply synergistic: a faithful world model provides the simulation substrate on which embodied systems can be trained and stress-tested at scale, while real-world experience gathered by embodied systems grounds and corrects the world model; embodied foundation models unify both by scaling a single architecture across data, compute, and embodiment. Our long-term vision is a physical AGI that understands, simulates, and acts in the world — safely and efficiently.
